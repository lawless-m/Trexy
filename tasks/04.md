Secondary layer-1 fault, datasheet-confirmed during planning. In /home/matt/Git/Trexy/crates/vectrex/src/via.rs, Via::step_shift shifts one bit per E cycle in the phi2-controlled modes (ACR bit 3 set) and one bit per two cycles otherwise. The W65C22 datasheet (https://eater.net/datasheets/w65c22.pdf, section 2.12.3 Figure 2-7 shift-in under PHI2, and section 2.13.3 Figure 2-11 shift-out under PHI2, mode 110) shows each bit occupying TWO PHI2 cycles — the CB1 shift clock runs at PHI2/2. In-machine corroboration: the BIOS print loop reloads the SR every 18 cycles per 8-bit glyph row — 8 bits x 2 cycles = 16 cycles of shifting plus 2 cycles slack fills the character cell exactly; at the current 1 bit/cycle the glyph occupies only the first 8 cycles of each 18-cycle cell and CB2 then holds the last glyph bit for 10 cycles, halving glyph width and smearing the last pixel. The T2-controlled modes (datasheet 2.13.1/2.13.2, Figures 2-9/2-10) show a CB1 half-period of N+2 cycles where N is the T2 latch low byte, i.e. 2*(N+2) cycles per bit.

Change step_shift so: phi2 modes (ACR bit 3 set) shift one bit per two E cycles; T2 modes shift one bit per 2*((t2_latch & 0xFF)+2) E cycles (with t2_latch=0 that is 4 cycles per bit, still strictly slower than phi2). Rewrite the step_shift doc comment to describe the actual cadence and cite the datasheet figures — do not leave the old claim that phi2 shifts every cycle (user CLAUDE.md: docs describe how things are now). The external-CB1-clock modes (011/111) are not used by the Vectrex; leave them grouped with phi2 as they are today — do not build CB1 input support.

Update the affected tests in via.rs to the new cadence: in shift_tests — `the_phi2_shift_clock_is_the_e_clock` (rename appropriately; 8 bits now take 16 cycles), `eight_shifts_present_the_pattern_most_significant_first` (the shift_out helper samples sr_output every cycle, so sample per two cycles or dedupe), `completing_eight_shifts_raises_the_flag` (16 steps), `rewriting_the_register_restarts_the_pattern` (a fresh write is 16 cycles), `the_pattern_survives_being_shifted_out` (32 steps for two full rotations), `the_timer_shift_clock_is_slower_than_the_e_clock` (T2 mode must remain strictly slower than the phi2 mode — adjust the assertion to compare against 16 if needed). Confirm control_tests `the_shift_register_takes_cb2_while_it_runs` and the frontend test `the_shift_register_chops_the_beam_into_dashes` in /home/matt/Git/Trexy/crates/vectrex/src/frontend.rs still pass (40 cycles of 0xAA now yields 20 shifts — still at least 3 lit and 3 dark spans); adjust their step counts only if an assertion genuinely requires it.
